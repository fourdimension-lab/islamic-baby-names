Islamic Naming Etiquette & Guidance

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ taught: "On the Day of Resurrection, you will be called by your names and the names of your fathers, so have good names." (Sunan Abi Dawud).

Recommended names include names of servitude to Allah (Abdullah, Abdur-Rahman), names of the Prophets, and names carrying righteous, uplifting virtues.
